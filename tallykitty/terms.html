Access

This public website provides information about the app. It does not offer account registration, app access or a public hosted invoicing service. Access to the private installation requires the operator's authorization.

Invoices and email

The owner is responsible for reviewing invoice details, tax settings, amounts, payment information, recipients and message content before finalizing an invoice or confirming an email send. Dashboard estimates do not replace professional accounting or tax advice.

Connecting Gmail is optional. Saving a Gmail draft leaves it unsent; sending requires a separate explicit action. Google acceptance of an email does not guarantee delivery. After an uncertain attempt, check Gmail before retrying to avoid duplicate messages.

Data and service availability

The operator maintains the private installation and its backups. Gmail features depend on Google's service and a valid authorization; authorization can expire or be revoked. See the privacy policy for data handling and disconnect/deletion information.
