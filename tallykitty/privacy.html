Data practices

Privacy policy

This policy covers the owner-operated TallyKitty installation and its optional Gmail integration.

TallyKitty is made and operated by Juju for private invoicing. Contact: madebyjujube@gmail.com. It is not currently a public sign-up service.

Data the app handles

The app stores the invoice and client information entered by its owner, including names, contact and billing details, invoice line items, amounts, payment records, settings and generated documents.

When the owner connects Gmail, TallyKitty receives the account's verified email address, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and token expiry information. The email address identifies the connected sender in Settings and the email composer.

Gmail permissions and purpose

TallyKitty requests userinfo.email to identify the sending account and gmail.compose to create unsent Gmail drafts with invoice PDFs attached and to send emails after an explicit owner review and confirmation. Google's compose permission also permits reading and managing drafts; the current app uses it to create drafts and send messages, and does not import, search or analyze inbox messages or existing draft contents.

When the owner chooses Save Gmail draft or confirms Send, TallyKitty transmits the chosen recipient, subject, message and attached finalized invoice PDF to Google. Sending also discloses that content to the selected recipients through Gmail. Gmail returns a message or draft identifier; the app records that identifier and the attempt's status to help prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

Storage and security

Invoice records and email attempt history are stored on the owner's private server. Gmail credentials are stored separately from business records in a persistent file with restricted filesystem permissions. They are excluded from the app's business exports and document synchronization. Google connections use HTTPS, and access to the private app is restricted to the owner through its private network and access controls.

Business records and documents can also exist in owner-controlled exports, synchronized copies and backups. Google stores drafts and sent messages under the connected Google account according to its own services and settings. OAuth credentials are not made available through this public website.

Sharing and limits on use

Google user data is used only for the connected-account identity, draft and sending features described here. TallyKitty does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. It does not transfer Gmail data to an analytics service. Access by another person for support or diagnosis requires the owner's authorization, except where necessary for security or required by law.

TallyKitty's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Gmail tokens are kept while the connection is configured and updated when refreshed. Settings → Email → Disconnect Gmail deletes the local token file and attempts to revoke Google's authorization. If revocation cannot be completed, the owner can remove TallyKitty's access from Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not erase existing invoices, attempt history, Gmail drafts or sent emails.

Invoice records and email attempt history are retained until the owner removes the relevant data or installation. Backups and exported copies may retain earlier records until the owner deletes or expires those copies. Existing Gmail drafts and sent messages can be managed or deleted directly in Gmail; deleting a local record cannot recall email already delivered. For data access, correction or deletion assistance, contact the operator at the address above.

This public information website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no forms, tracking scripts, analytics integrations or advertising. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details to serve and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. No invoices, OAuth tokens or private app records are hosted here.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. Material changes to Google data access or use will be disclosed before asking the owner to authorize those changes.
