TallyKitty

An invoice app for creating PDFs, keeping client records and tracking what has been billed and paid.

TallyKitty is currently a private, self-hosted app used by its owner. This public website explains the app and its data practices. It does not provide access to invoices or accept registrations.

What the app does

  • Creates English and French invoices, with client details, line items and payment information.
  • Generates invoice PDFs and keeps bookkeeping records.
  • Shows billed, paid and outstanding amounts.

Optional Gmail connection

The owner can connect a Google account to save an invoice email as an unsent Gmail draft with its PDF attached, or explicitly review and send it from the app. Editable payment reminders are also available. There are no scheduled or automatic email sends.

The Google connection identifies the sending account and requests permission to manage drafts and send messages. TallyKitty does not import or search the Gmail inbox.

Read the privacy policy for the data accessed, its use, storage and deletion, and the terms of use for the current private-use scope.
